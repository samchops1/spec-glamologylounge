What I offer

Color first. Then the cut that lives with you.

A little about what I offer in the lounge. New clients start with a consultation — I will not book a first service without one.

One process

Single Process Coloring

A root touch-up or root-to-end all over. No foils, no hand painting — one process, and I can still use more than one color at once.

Gradient

Ombres and Color Melting

Darkest to lightest, or many tones melted so they read as one. Both usually need prelightening.

Double process

Blonding

Prelightening and glossing — all-over, foils, or hand painting — to land a specific tone.

Fantasy

Vivids, Mermaids, and Unicorns

They all mean the same thing: magical hair. A full head or a pop of color, usually more than one process.

Shape & repair

Haircuts and Treatments

Cuts tailored to your head shape, hair type, and life. Treatments ride along with every chemical service, no extra charge.
