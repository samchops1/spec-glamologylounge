Let's change things up

A little about what I offer.

These are the services I print on my page. Consultations are required for all new clients, prior to any booked service.

One process · no foils

Single Process Coloring

This is a basic salon color application. Whether it's a root touch-up or a root to end all over, it's one process with no foils or hand painting. Multiple colors can be used simultaneously to create ultra-dimensional results.

Gradient · usually prelightened

Ombres and Color Melting

Both ombre and color melt techniques produce essentially the same result: a gradient of one color to another. An ombre generally blends colors from darkest to lightest while a color melt blends many tones cohesively. Usually both require prelightening to achieve results.

Double process

Blonding

This is called a double process service because it requires prelightening and glossing to achieve specific tonal results. Whether it's an all-over, or a highlight with foils, or hand painting, this usually requires double or multiple color processes to acquire your goal.

Fantasy · multiple processes

Vivids, Mermaids, and Unicorns

They all mean the same thing — magical hair. If you're looking for a full head or just a pop of color, this usually requires multiple processes to achieve a fantasy look. Unless your hair is at target level, this service requires prelightening prior to vivid application.

Personalized · treatments included

Haircuts and Treatments

The treatments I offer help to strengthen and repair damage caused by natural elements, heat styling, and chemical processing. I offer treatments in every chemical service with no additional charge, however, I do offer stand alone treatments as well. All of my haircuts are personalized and tailored to you, as I take into consideration your head shape, hair type, and lifestyle requirements.

New to the lounge?

I will not book a first service until we have sat for a consultation. Call or write and tell me what you want the hair to do.
