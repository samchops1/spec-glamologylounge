Let me introduce myself

Hi, I'm Jessica!

A stylist with over fifteen years' experience. This is the chair I built twice — once in Florida, then again here.

I started my career when I was just seventeen. Not knowing I was embarking on a journey that would change my life forever, I enrolled in cosmetology school and lost myself in the curriculum. It was the most fun I'd ever had and before I knew it, I was licensed by the state of Florida.

I immediately found a chair and began building my book. It didn't take long before I knew I'd made the right career choice; I was happy and so were my guests.

At least, I thought I was happy. One trip to Colorado changed my perspective on home and I found myself relocating across the country shortly thereafter. Now that I've been living here I couldn't imagine going anywhere else.

After becoming established as a licensed hairstylist here in Colorado, I began building my career from the bottom up for the second time in my life. Come join me on this journey of growth, inspiration, and discovery and together we can make Colorado even more beautiful.
